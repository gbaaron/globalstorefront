Why I started this

A lot of great local businesses (bakeries, restaurants, barbershops, corner shops) end up with a generic template or a page that barely does anything. It looks fine, but it can't take an order, book an appointment, or reward a regular. Global Storefront is my answer to that.

Every site is built by hand in real code: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Airtable, Netlify. No drag-and-drop builders, no theme kits. The result is a site made for your shop specifically: your menu, your hours, your customers, and the way you actually run the place.

What I actually build

This isn't a pretty homepage with nothing behind it. Here's the real work that goes into every shop I take on:

Hand-coded, from a blank file

Every page is written from scratch, so nothing is bloated, broken, or borrowed from a template the shop down the street is also using.

Online ordering & scheduling that works

Real order-ahead carts, appointment booking, and payment processing. Working systems your customers actually use, not screenshots of a feature you'll get later.

Rewards & loyalty for your regulars

Points on every purchase and Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers that bring people back. The kind of loyalty a punch card can't track.

An owner dashboard with the real numbers

Manage orders, track inventory, and see what's actually selling. A back office built for you, not vanity stats you can't use.

A smart helper bot on every site

A built-in assistant that answers your customers' questions in your voice (menu, hours, and policies) day or night.

Free preview before you pay a cent

I spec it, build a live version you can log into and click around, and you only pay when it's ready to go live on your own domain.
